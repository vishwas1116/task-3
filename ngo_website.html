Our Story

Started with a Vision,
Grown with Heart

Founded in 2018, Udaan Foundation began with a simple belief — every person deserves access to education, healthcare, and dignity. Today we operate across India with certified programs and a passionate volunteer network.

What We Do

Our Programs

Structured, community-driven programs designed to create lasting systemic change.

🏫

Community Learning Centers

Free education centers in rural and semi-urban areas for children aged 5–16 who lack access to quality schooling.

🍱

Daily Meal Distribution

Hot meals distributed daily to underprivileged families, migrant workers, and homeless individuals.

🌳

Green India Initiative

Mass tree plantation drives and environment awareness campaigns with schools and local communities.

👩‍💼

Women Skill Training

Short-term vocational courses in stitching, beauty, computer basics, and entrepreneurship for women.

🐕

Animal Rescue & Care

Emergency rescue, veterinary care, and feeding programs for stray animals across partnered cities.

💼

Youth Career Support

Resume building, interview prep, and job placement assistance for first-generation job seekers.
